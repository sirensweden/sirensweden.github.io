Welcome to SiREN Signal 2026

SiREN Signal 2026 will be held on May 27th to May 28th from lunch to lunch at Mälardalens Universitet in Västerås, Sweden.

Quick Facts

  • When: May 27, 12:00, until May 28, 13:00
  • Where: Ypsilon (U2-020)

More information will follow soon. To stay on track, follow our LinkedIn page or contact the steering committee to be added to our mailing list.
